01 About

Curious mind.
Building with purpose.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering student at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB), with a foundation shaped at Dhaka Residential Model College (DRMC). I care deeply about how systems work — from the logic of digital circuits to the elegance of well-structured code.

My interests sit at the intersection of digital systems and competitive programming. I enjoy the precision of hardware simulation and the problem-solving intensity of algorithms. Outside academics, I run TechSubFlix, where I make premium streaming simpler for people in Bangladesh.

I believe good engineering is quiet, reliable, and human-centered. Whether simulating a home automation system or solving a hard programming problem, I aim for clarity and correctness.
